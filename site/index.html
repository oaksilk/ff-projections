Proj is the average outcome. The range bar shows the 10th–90th percentile, with the darker box covering the middle half and a tick at the median. Boom is the chance of 20+ points.

Why shows how many points each factor adds or subtracts compared with a neutral baseline. Click a player for details, then pick a second player to see who is more likely to score more.

What the factors mean

Each factor is measured the same way: the model re-runs the player's projection with that factor reset to a neutral baseline, and the difference (in half-PPR points) is the factor's effect. There are no fixed weights; the model learned from ten seasons of games how much each input matters, and the effect depends on the player (a factor can matter more for a pass-catcher than for a running back). Everything not listed here, mainly the player's own role and production, is the baseline projection itself.
